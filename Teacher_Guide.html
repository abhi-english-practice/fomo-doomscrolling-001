FOMO · Three Levels

10 shared pictures · 70 questions per level · 210 Q&A.

Three-step flow

  1. Build & Say: assemble all shuffled chunks, check, then say the sentence.
  2. Look & Answer: answer with short hints.
  3. Speak on Your Own: answer without hints, model, context or explanation.

Complete group A (4 observation questions) through steps 1–3, then group B (3 first-person story questions) through steps 1–3. Continue to the next picture.

Assessment

Built /70 records correct model assembly. Self-practice /140 records learner confirmation in steps 2–3. Neither measures pronunciation or speaking quality. Spoken answers may use other grammatical wording; teachers assess meaning and target language.

Language and context

Observation uses present forms and cautious interpretations. Group B asks learners to take the narrator’s role. Read the scene context before practicing. A2 uses foundational patterns; B1 and B2+ develop questions and answers using useful source language. Level labels describe task design, not verified CEFR classifications for individual words.

Final Q&A

Answers remain hidden until each learner selects Show answer. Changing level, changing filter or reopening Final Q&A closes answers. Vietnamese notes are off by default.

Tiếng Việt

Hướng dẫn học sinh xem tranh, đọc câu hỏi, ghép cụm và nói lại; sau đó giảm hỗ trợ qua từng bước. Điểm lưu riêng từng cấp trên trình duyệt. Các bối cảnh minh họa do giáo viên cung cấp; không xem suy đoán từ tranh là dữ kiện chắc chắn. Năm tranh đầu về FOMO; năm tranh sau về Doomscrolling.
